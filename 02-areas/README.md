# Areas

Ongoing responsibilities without an end date. They need to stay healthy, but they are never "done".

The five areas here are examples. Rename, remove, or add areas so they fit your business. Each area has a `README.md` (current state) and, after processing, a `GENERATED.md` and a `sources/` folder.

A lead or a proposal for one specific client is not part of the Sales area. It is a project in `01-projects/seeding/`.
